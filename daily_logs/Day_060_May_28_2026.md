## The two negative results (Packs 154 + 155)

Honest science. Both were attempts to scale Pack 153's 200-noun curriculum overlay to 1000 nouns and recover the lost top-3 accuracy.

- **Pack 154**: 1000-noun overlay regressed top-3 from 56% to 44%. Hypothesized bank saturation.
- **Pack 155**: Frequency filter on candidates -- didn't help, because the contaminating tokens (aunt, mother, consumer, striker) are themselves common-frequency words.
- **Pack 156**: Doubled M_rel to 16384 -- didn't help either; bank size wasn't the bottleneck.
- **Pack 157**: per-role gamma sweep -- **the real fix**. With property-dominant weighting (`isa: 0.1, property: 4.0, rest: 0`), the same saturated 1000-noun substrate jumps to 78% top-3, matching the clean 26-noun Pack 150 baseline.

The lesson: when the substrate looks broken, check the readout weights before changing the substrate. The geometry was there all along; the cleanup was just selecting the wrong axis.

## Process notes

- **Pack 154-156 negative results** documented honestly. Each was attempt to fix Pack 153's saturated 1000-noun overlay; each failed because the actual bottleneck was elsewhere. Pack 157's gamma sweep proved that the substrate had the right geometry all along.
- **Pack 158 hit 100% top-3** only after fixing two implementation issues: (1) property anchors needed their own antonym+self-property structure so subtracting them actually removes property mass; (2) the ANTONYM category needs role-direct query, not concept-neighbour query (concepts push AWAY from antonyms via gamma -0.5).
- **Pack 162 V8 ("axiom removal breaks downstream inference")** required building a fresh reasoning engine without the foundational fact and re-running. Just unrelating wasn't enough -- the LogicalFixedPoint engine cached derived facts; the test had to verify a clean rerun.
- **Pack 166 + 167 confidence/magnitude tests** were repeatedly thwarted by VSASDM.read()'s renorm-on-read. Solution: bypass the renorm by reading C[locs] directly. Pre-renorm magnitude is the substrate's TRUE confidence signal.
