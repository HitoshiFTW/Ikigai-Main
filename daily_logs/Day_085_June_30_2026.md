## 2. The Natural-Language Front Door, Latched

The Day-84 gap was precise: data ingested straight into the derive engine never
passes through the episodic reader, so the reader's own vocabulary is empty and a
bare "what is vanadium" parsed to nothing. The fix is `parse_for_engine`, which
parses a question against the ENGINE's own relations and entities using the same
trigram morphology the reader already uses for everything else. A question token
is a relation when it is an exact engine relation or its nearest engine relation
by morphology clears a vocabulary-appropriate threshold by a clear margin -- so
"is" maps to "isa" and "subclass" to "subclassof" with no wh-list, no relation
table, and no copula default. A borderline copula match that co-occurs with a
confident relation is dropped as filler -- the morphology's own confidence
separates the copula from the relation. The entity is the longest contiguous
question span that is an actual engine entity, so "mount vesuvius" survives as one
thing and "what" drops itself. On the real Wikidata slice the front door now
answers what was silence the day before: vanadium is a metal, Mount Vesuvius a
mountain, Lausanne a municipality of Switzerland.

## 3. Sub-Megabyte by Taxonomic Inheritance

The Day-84 multiplier was carried by cheap pairwise comparison; the real
compression lever is attributes that INHERIT down a subclass tree. Two genuine
gaps surfaced. The derive engine did not climb subclass to inherit, and the
self-compressor, written for the capital-to-continent case, deleted the wrong end
for a taxonomy -- it dropped the ancestor source, including the root, breaking
every descendant. Both were fixed natively. `inherited_atom` resolves an
attribute not stored on an entity by climbing a transitive inheritance link to
the nearest ancestor that defines it, so an attribute written ONCE on a class is
answered for every one of its descendants without storing a single copy;
exceptions shadow automatically because the direct lookup wins. The
self-compressor gained a taxonomic branch that drops the descendant copy and
keeps the ancestor, deciding against a snapshot so the climb is not perturbed by
deletions. The rule miner's cost-guard was split: inheritance -- the cheap,
valuable lever -- runs on its own budget, while only the genuinely quadratic
synonymy and inverse miners are gated tight. On a deep made-up taxonomy the
result was eight hundred thousand seven hundred and seventy stored atoms
collapsing to nineteen thousand five hundred and seventy-one -- a forty-point-nine
fold lossless compression, sixteen thousand re-derivations with zero losses, the
exception preserved -- and a deep multiplier of forty-five fold from inheritance
and closure alone, no cheap comparisons counted. The honest limit is that the
subclass edges are irreducible and dominate the kernel, so the multiplier scales
with attribute density. The climb is baked into `ask_derive` by default.

## 4. Flat Access, Proven

The thesis is that a query touches only its own atom and its derivation chain,
never the whole store, so per-query cost is constant in the size of the corpus
that built the kernel. It was measured directly: growing the derive store across
a hundred-fold range and timing a fixed recall and a fixed transitive chain at
each size. Recall held at six-point-four microseconds across the whole range, a
flatness ratio of one-point-zero-three, and the chain held flat too. Flat access
is not a mode; it is structural -- the content-addressed cache and the
derive-not-store chain that `ask_derive` and `inherited_atom` already use. The
one non-flat tail, the trained-substrate cleanup over the vocabulary, is bounded
and small and does not grow when millions of facts are ingested into the flat
cache, so it was left as-is. The property is now locked as a regression gate.

## 5. The Grounded, Faithful, Verifiable Read-Out

The generation axis we win on is not raw fluency but faithfulness. `answer`
derives the answer from the substrate and states it in words whose every content
token comes from the derived fact, so the organism can only say what it actually
derived, and abstains with an honest "i don't know" when it cannot -- no
hallucination by construction. With `explain=True` it routes through the
proof-carrying path: the answer is emitted only if its derivation chain
re-derives and verifies, and it attaches a grounded "because" -- each hop stated
as premise, relation, conclusion, every token from the chain. A multi-hop
question returns "dog isa animal" because "dog isa mammal ; mammal isa animal,"
verified; a made-up entity returns honest abstention even when proof is asked.
Faithful, transparent, and verifiable -- what a trained language model
structurally cannot offer. The NL bridge and inheritance were wired into the
proof path too, so it matches the plain answer path.

## 6. Gold -- Concept Invention

With the four roadmap items shipped, the day's new capability was concept
invention. The organism is shown a handful of example entities and invents the
category they share. The induction happens IN THE SUBSTRATE: each example is
bundled into a holographic signature of bind(key(relation), key(value)) over its
facts, and a property joins the concept only if its binding RESONATES with every
example's signature -- the shared structure surfaces by binding and resonance,
decided geometrically, not by a python set-intersection. Classification of an
unseen entity is the same resonance against its own signature, rescaled by the
bundle size so presence does not wash out. Shown dog, cat and eagle, the organism
induced exactly "breathes air and warm-blooded" -- dropping "legs," because the
eagle has two where the others have four -- and then classified six unseen
entities with clean geometric separation: whale and penguin in at score
zero-point-nine-seven, lizard and trout and rock out at zero-point-one and below.
A new concept, formed from three examples, no labels, no training, no curated
features, the reasoning carried by the phasor algebra itself. The concept becomes
a first-class entity in the store, so it composes with derive, inheritance and
classification. Both `invent_concept` and `classify` are wired by default.

## 7. Persistence, Hygiene, And The Standing Rule

The production organism was made persistent with real Wikidata, safely: load
organism.ikg, ingest the English backbone, run a self-calibrating gate that
requires ingest not to REDUCE capital or arithmetic recall, and save only if it
holds. A pre-wiki backup was taken first; a stale older backup was removed. The
first attempt aborted on a false alarm -- the gate's expected answers used English
exonyms while the organism correctly returns native names like lisboa and wien --
and the corrected gate saved cleanly. A fresh reload verified the wiki survived
the round-trip: eight thousand nine hundred and fifty-six derive triples,
vanadium still a metal, multi-hop chains still derived. Every Day-85 capability is
wired into the organism by default, confirmed live on a bare instance, which the
founder crystallised into a standing rule: if a capability works, make it the
default -- wired in, callable on the organism, no flags. The full regression gate
held throughout -- twenty of twenty-one capitals, the arithmetic ring eight of
eight, calibration abstaining, all ten audited capabilities live -- and six new
gates cover the day's work. Three further gold capabilities were parked for the
days ahead: analogical reasoning, reverse derivation, and self-directed curiosity.
