## 5. Emergent Word->Int Grounding via Parallel Arithmetic Frames

Pack 253 cat-3 absorb has, since Day 73, scanned reasoning chains for 5-token arithmetic frames `[num_a, T_op, num_b, T_eq, num_c]` and bound `T_op` to canonical operator HVs whenever the arithmetic verifies. Day 74 proved this mechanism with alien operator words ("zorked", "splork") at 100% on synthetic chains.

Day 75 extends the same mechanism to ground word-form number magnitudes from data alone, no word->int lookup table. The mechanism scans each chain for parallel digit-arithmetic AND all-word arithmetic frames sharing the same op-slot and eq-slot tokens. When found, bind each word-form number to the FPE magnitude HV of the positionally-aligned digit-form number.

```
chain : "5 plus 3 equals 8 . five plus three equals eight"

frame A (digits): [5, plus, 3, equals, 8] verifies 5+3=8
frame B (words):  [five, plus, three, equals, eight] aligns by op+eq tokens

bind: five -> FPE(5)
bind: three -> FPE(3)
bind: eight -> FPE(8)
```

No predefined number-word list. The mapping emerges from positional structure of the chain itself.

Smoke on 300 synthetic chains (10 unique chains x 30 reps): word recall 12/15 = 80%. The 3 failures were off-by-one at small numbers (one->2, two->3, three->4) caused by Pack 252 FPE decoder noise at low magnitudes when scale=10 gives only 100 reliable integers per decode grid.

**The fix landed same day**: swap FPE -> RHC for magnitude encoding in `Cat3Absorb.__init__`. Default `mag_encoder='rhc'` instantiates a Pack 257 ResidueHDC with moduli (7,11,13,17). Recall and decode go through `_decode_magnitude` which calls RHC.decode (brute mode over the range). Word recall after swap: **15/15 = 100%**.

This is the substrate-side complement to Pack 257 RHC's encoding-side capacity. RHC encodes integers; emergent grounding ingests natural language; together they retire the substrate's hardcoded word->int dependence entirely.

## 12. Pack 262 v6/v7 -- The Prototype-Collapse Discovery and Trailing-Weighted Focus_HV

Bench on the populated 1500-chain b_self with v5:
- "What is the capital of France" -> 'paris' CORRECT (sim 0.826)
- "What is the capital of Japan" -> 'paris' WRONG (sim 0.638)
- "What is the capital of Germany" -> 'paris' WRONG (sim 0.631)
- "What is the capital of Spain" -> 'paris' WRONG (sim 0.631)
- "What is the capital of Italy" -> 'paris' WRONG (sim 0.610)

**Prototype collapse.** Multiple capital-of-X queries all returned 'paris' even with different country tokens in the state. Diagnosis: `focus_hv` was uniform-weighted across the window -- common framing tokens (what, is, the, capital, of) dominated the state HV, and the unique country token contributed only 1/6 of the signal. Pairwise cosine between "capital of France" and "capital of Germany" focus HVs: **0.83** (way too similar).

Sharp softmax (beta=20 vs beta=4) did not help -- it sharpened the softmax over a nearly-uniform input distribution.

**The architectural fix (v7):** trailing-weighted `focus_hv`. Each token's contribution to the state HV gets multiplied by an exponential decay factor from the end: last token weight 1.0, previous 0.5, 0.25, 0.125, etc. Effective window ~ 4 trailing content-bearing tokens.

```python
for i, t in enumerate(toks):
    decay = 0.5 ** (n - 1 - i)  # last token gets 1.0
    hv = self.mr.ck.key(t)
    accum += decay * self.pik.pi(i % n_primes, hv)
```

Empirical separation post-fix:
- France vs Germany: 0.83 -> **0.13**
- France vs Japan: 0.83 -> 0.06
- France vs Spain: 0.83 -> 0.02
- France vs Italy: 0.83 -> 0.04
- France vs math state: 0.07 (cross-archetype, correctly low)

**Clean separation between specific instances within an archetype.**

Re-absorbed 1500 chains with new encoder. Post-fix bench: "Capital of France" -> 'paris' (sim 0.523), "Capital of Germany" -> 'berlin' (sim 0.352). Japan/Spain/Italy below threshold (0.08-0.13) -- the teacher seed prompts apparently focused on European capitals and didn't cover Asian/specific others strongly enough. Math queries fall to next_token because Pack 254 isn't wired in yet.
