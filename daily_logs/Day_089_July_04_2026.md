## 1. Objective

Day 88 closed on a plan to push the generation loop toward real fluency. Day 89's
objective was reshaped in conversation. The founder's target was blunt: the framework must
have the ABILITY, given data, to produce a large coherent artifact -- a long program, a
long essay -- with correct grammar, tone, and fluency, without hardcoding, fully emergent,
and without hitting representation walls. Not a narrow code tool. A general organism that
understands what is asked and does it, at any scale from a small chat to a large document.
The standing rules held throughout, and were tested harder than usual: no hardcoding, no
cheating (the operation must BE a substrate op), pixel-perfect on the production path, and
above all no glazing.

---

## 2. Multi-Constraint Generation: Composition That Scales

Day 88 proved the generation loop holds ONE constraint (a theme) over length where a local
walker drifts. One constraint is a lab demo; a real sentence satisfies many at once. Day 89
extended `org.generate_fluent` to compose N constraints by product-of-experts
(`combine='poe'`, the default), and added `org.bundle_constraint(tokens)` -- a constraint
built as a phasor bundle of a category's substrate keys, scored by cosine resonance, so
membership in one axis is independent of the others. Measured on made-up alpha tokens over
three independent axes (`experiments/nl/day89_multi_constraint.py`): one critic pins its
axis to 100 percent and leaves the others free; composing 1, 2, then 3 critics pins exactly
those axes with ZERO degradation, while a local baseline drifts to 3 percent. Every step
grounded, the whole sequence novel. Understanding a request as a set of composed
constraints is the mechanism, and it scales.

---

## 3. The Scope Register, and Dropping a Framing That Smelled Wrong

The first attempt at "large-artifact ability" built a correct-by-construction structured
generator: a made-up program grammar with declare and use tokens, and a rule that a use is
only valid if the symbol was declared earlier, enforced by a dynamic substrate set register
(`org.scope_register`, a hierarchical bundle that holds membership at scale where a flat
bundle drowns in crosstalk). The register itself is a clean, general VSA primitive and it
stays. But the founder caught the framing: the declare/use grammar and the hand-written
"use needs declare" rule were authored, code-specific scaffolding -- exactly the hardcoding
we forbid. The gate was not run and not claimed. The lesson was kept: the general capability
is not "code scope," it is long-range consistency, and the constraint must be induced from
data, not typed by hand. The register and the new loop hooks (`veto`, `on_emit`) remain as
general, inert-by-default primitives.

---

## 4. The Reframe: One Emergent Loop, Not a Menu of Modes

Pressed further, the shape became clear and was stated back plainly. The organism should
perceive a request, understand it as the request resonating into constraints (no parser, no
intent labels), work out what would satisfy it (the gap, measured as free energy), and act
to close that gap -- retrieve, derive, generate, or plan -- choosing which by surprise, not
by a hardcoded router. A small chat and a large artifact are the same loop at different
depth. Goal, plan, execute, revise is not a separate machine; it is the deep end of that one
loop. This is the honest north star, and it pointed at a specific place in the existing code.

---

## 5. Finding the Real Hardcoding

The organism already answers a free-form request today, through `org.ask` into
`GeneralReasoner.reason`. Inside it, the detectors are genuinely emergent: operator
detection is a substrate resonance with no operator-word list, and abstention is the
geometric noise floor with no tuned threshold. But the top-level routing -- which method
fires -- was a hand-authored if/elif PRIORITY LADDER, at two sites: the fast path
(`_fast_answer`) and the slow ladder inside `reason`. Arithmetic, then derive, then
multi-hop, then exact cache, then active learning, then soft recall, then planner, then
abstain, then next-token, in a fixed typed order. Add a capability and you edit the ladder.
That fixed order was the hardcoding the founder kept sensing.

---

## 6. Emergent Free-Energy Dispatch

The replacement: every path already carries a confidence, so give each a free energy
F = -log(confidence) and pick argmin F -- a uniform scalar competition instead of a typed
order. Exact paths (arithmetic, derive, multi-hop, exact cache) sit at F = 0 and dominate;
a soft recall competes at F = -log(similarity) but only above the calibrated acceptance
boundary; the planner ranks below any accepted recall; nothing below the geometric noise
floor is emitted, which is abstention. Two pieces of the old ladder were preserved on
purpose because they are principled rather than arbitrary: the eligibility gate that lets
the organism ask a teacher when uncertain (the epistemic, explore action of expected free
energy) and the geometric abstain boundary. The fixed method ORDER is gone; a new capability
now joins by registering a proposer. Implemented in `general_reasoner.py` as `_arbitrate`,
`_free_energy`, `_fast_answer_fe`, and a wrapped slow ladder, all gated by `_fe_dispatch`.

---

## 7. Pixel-Perfect Discipline

The reasoner is the production path, guarded by the regression gate. The change was built
behind a flag so that flag-off is byte-identical to the old code, and a pixel harness
(`experiments/nl/day89_dispatch_pixel.py`) recorded per-query answer and method for the
ladder and for the arbiter across 36 queries spanning capitals, arithmetic, multi-hop,
abstention, and generic text. The arbiter reproduced the ladder with zero answer
differences and zero method-label changes. Only then was the flag flipped to make the
arbiter the default.

---

## 8. Regression

With the arbiter as default and `organism.ikg` only loaded, never saved: the full
capability battery held at capitals 20/21, arithmetic 8/8, honest abstention on a made-up
entity, and 10/10 wired capabilities, at about 1.0 ms per query. The pixel harness stayed a
perfect match. The Day-88 gates held: the reasoning arena at 100 percent through 8 hops with
zero fabrications, free energy monotone to zero, multi-constraint composition green, and the
generation loop back-compatible and green. The flag and the harness remain for instant
rollback.

## 10. Honest Reckoning

What changed today is the SELECTION, not the capabilities. The organism answers the same
questions with the same answers; it now chooses how to answer by a uniform measure of
surprise rather than by a hand-written order, which removes a real piece of hardcoding and
makes the dispatch extensible and scale-free. That is a genuine step toward the emergent
understand-and-respond loop, and it is the foundation the depth and scale of that loop will
build on. It is not, by itself, fluent long-form generation, and it does not close the gap
on large open-ended output, which the frontier still wins. Multi-constraint composition is a
mechanism at small scale on made-up tokens, not an essay engine. Both are said plainly.

## 11. Next

Build depth and scale on the emergent dispatch: let the same arbiter reach for the planner
and the generation loop on a large request the way it reaches for derive on a small one, so
that goal, plan, execute, and revise fall out of the one loop rather than a separate path.
Induce long-range consistency from data instead of authoring it, so the register enforces
what the organism learned rather than a typed rule. In parallel, for the hunt: the clean
real-ontology multiplier, re-fetched, and the proof-of-concept one-pager kept in step with
the website. The rule is unchanged: measure, do not claim; wire what works on by default;
keep the production path pixel-perfect; and never glaze a loss.
