## 2. The Arena: Equal-Knowledge Reasoning

The first instinct was a head-to-head benchmark. The organism knows almost
nothing next to a frontier model that ate the internet, so a knowledge test is a
loss and we do not pretend otherwise. But reasoning is a different axis. The arena
(`experiments/nl/day88_arena.py`) hands the SAME facts to both sides and scores who
DERIVES the answer as the hop count grows -- exactly how ProofWriter, CLUTRR and
bAbI are scored. On a synthetic taxonomy of 30/depth chains, the organism derived
100% correct multi-hop membership through 8 hops (700 held-out facts, never stored,
derived on demand), fabricated nothing on 199 negatives and 200 unknown entities,
and cost 9 content-addressed lookups and ~76 microseconds per query on one CPU
core. That is our half of the head-to-head, and it is clean.

---

## 3. The Real Head-to-Head -- and the Artifact We Caught

The founder authorised running a live frontier model (overriding the usual
"only run Ikigai" rule) to fill the opponent column with a measured number, not an
annotation. Against `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b` (free, via OpenRouter), same
items, same grading, shuffled facts plus distractors: the frontier matched us at
100% on hops 1-7, then showed 0% at 8 hops.

That 0% looked like the win we wanted -- which is exactly why we distrusted it. The
run reported 9 API errors of 62 calls and a 60-second average latency; the hop-8
bucket was only 4 calls. A clean re-run to verify came back 429 (rate-limited) on
all six attempts, confirming the diagnosis: the "0%" was rate-limit noise landing
in a tiny bucket, not a reasoning failure. We threw the number out. The honest
verdict: at this difficulty it is an accuracy TIE -- a modern reasoning model chains
a followable is-a list fine; our old "0% at 8 hops" was a weaker, non-reasoning
model. Where we genuinely and enormously win is COMPUTE: 9 lookups and microseconds
on a CPU versus ~1,339 generated tokens and a full forward pass on a datacenter GPU.
The fundable claim shifts accordingly and truthfully: not "we out-reason it," but
"we match its correctness at a millionth the compute."

## 5. The Scaling Proof of Concept

`experiments/nl/day88_scaling.py`, every number measured on one CPU core with
seedable made-up data, and a contamination guard (`kept == stored`, so name
collisions crash instead of lying -- it caught exactly that bug on the first run):

- **Free to train.** 15,275 facts/sec/core; no GPU, no gradient steps. Projected:
  one billion facts in ~18 core-hours ~ **$0.36**. A frontier model of comparable
  coverage costs millions of GPU-dollars. This is the economic thesis in one line.
- **Flat compute.** 9 lookups per query at 1,801 atoms AND at 180,001 atoms -- 100x
  more knowledge, identical cost. Energy scales with the derivation CHAIN, not the
  amount of knowledge.
- **Footprint.** ~5.3 bytes per stored triple today; a MEASURED floor of **3.8 B**
  via columnar dictionary-encoding + bit-packing (turning a projection into a fact),
  and it improves with scale. One million facts ~ 3.8 MB. Below that is the Shannon
  information content of the triple; the only way past it is the multiplier.
- **Multiplier.** ~5x on this taxonomy (closure, honest); the prior real Wikidata
  backbone run answered billions from 1.88M atoms in 12.8 MB, but that headline
  number is comparison-inflated and is flagged as such.

Assembled into `day88_POC.md` -- the collaborator-facing one-pager, which states
plainly what it does NOT show: not a benchmark accuracy win, not large-scale
generation, and a multiplier whose loud number needs a clean real-ontology re-run.

## 6. The Dead-Module Audit

The founder suspected ~90% of the ~112 cognition modules never actually run. We
tested it (`experiments/nl/day88_dead_module_audit.py`): static (imported by any
file?) plus dynamic (a `sys.settrace` tracer over the organism's full capability
battery -- which functions actually execute?). The first result read 41% alive, but
the honesty rule caught the contamination: 13 of 62 battery calls errored on OUR
bad arguments, each one falsely marking its module dead. Corrected, the solid finding
is 10 modules imported by NOTHING (dead files), not 90%. The suspicion is
directionally right -- real dead and decorative code exists -- but an order of
magnitude smaller than feared; the organism's core genuinely runs. One byproduct
fed the next section: the free-energy modules (`fe_action`, `active_inference_planner`)
were among the barely-exercised.

## 7. Everything Runs on Free Energy

The founder asked to make the organism run on free energy. Honestly scoped: the
AUTONOMY runs on it; the reasoning stays exact derivation, and we do not pretend
derivation is free-energy minimisation. `org.free_energy(observations)` computes a
real quantity -- the mean surprise of a held-out probe under the current generative
model, from substrate derivation and calibrated belief confidence. Three claims,
measured (`experiments/nl/day88_free_energy.py`): LEARNING drives F from 4.61 to
0.00 monotonically as the entailing edges arrive and the probe becomes derivable;
PERCEPTION corrects a wrong belief and its surprise collapses to zero; ACTION is
chosen by EXPECTED free energy (explore = CuriosityDrive novelty + exploit = purpose),
dispatched through the previously-dead `FreeEnergyActionSelector.select_from_values`
-- a decorative module turned live. Wired by default: `contemplate` now ranks gaps by
EFE; `live()` can track F across a lifetime. Regression held.

## 8. The Biological Path to Winning Generation

Pressed on the one domain we lose -- full-richness language, where the LLM keeps the
tacit/distributional knowledge our clean triples discard -- we refused to concede
and looked where the founder pointed: biology. Biology generates fuzzy, creative
output with neither backprop nor symbolic rules. It uses a third path that is
exactly Ikigai's family: the immune system generates novel antibodies by VARIATION +
SELECTION; the cortex is a generative model running top-down by PREDICTIVE CODING;
the cerebellum (Kanerva's SDM, our flat memory) does RESONANCE / attractor cleanup;
synapses do one-shot Hebbian binding (in-context learning). We already hold every
mechanism: variation = `dream`, selection = `_validate` + the grounded derive-engine
critic, resonance = the resonator network, predictive coding = the free-energy
machinery built today, one-shot = flat memory. The directive is saved: win generation
the biological way, not by out-symboling the frontier.

## 9. Breaking the Representation-Capacity Wall

The concrete blocker for long-range generation is the Frady-Sommer crosstalk wall:
ONE flat superposition of dimension d holds only ~sqrt(d) items before recall
collapses. The mindset move -- biology never holds an essay in one activity vector --
is to route around Shannon with HIERARCHY, not to fight it. Measured
(`experiments/nl/day88_capacity_wall.py`): the same holographic memory, flat (one
bundle) versus hierarchical (bounded chunks of 8). Flat fidelity holds to ~24 items,
crosses below 95% at 48, and collapses to 8.6% at 256. Hierarchical holds 100% all
the way to 256 -- the wall is a property of one flat bundle, not of the substrate.
This wall-breaking codec is already native and default (`holo_writer`, chunk=8), so
the confirmation is a proof, not a new wire.

## 10. The Generation Loop -- Native, Default

On top of the bounded-chunk state we assembled the loop
(`experiments/nl/day88_generation_loop.py`, then native as `org.generate_fluent`,
gated by `day88_generate_fluent.py`). Each step: VARIATION = `valid_next`, the
grounded set of real observed transitions recovered by matching pursuit (a candidate
is selected, never invented); SELECTION = the flat co-occurrence substrate scores
each candidate by RESONANCE to a persistent theme anchor -- a coherence critic; STATE
= the anchor is held OUTSIDE the local window, so the long-range constraint survives
length. On a themed corpus with bridge tokens that let a local walker drift, the
local generator's theme-consistency fell from 86% to 40% by length 80; the loop held
100% at every length, every step a real transition (grounded, no hallucination), the
whole sequence novel. Wired by default (the coherence critic is the default path).
Regression -- arena and free-energy gates -- held after the `integrate.py` edit.

## 11. Honest Reckoning

The generation loop proves the MECHANISM -- long-range coherence via persistent state
over grounded variation with a distributional critic -- on one clean constraint and
toy vocabulary. It is not fluent essays. Real fluency needs many simultaneous
constraints, a richer coherence/syntax/discourse critic, the derive engine wired in
as a content critic so generated statements are factually grounded, and real
vocabulary at scale. On large open-ended generation the frontier still wins today,
and the accuracy of our reasoning at easy difficulty merely ties a modern model --
we say both plainly. What is now true and was not this morning: three of the four
generation pillars (tacit knowledge, long-range capacity, predictive coding) are
native and on by default, and the fourth -- the assembled loop -- exists in miniature.
