# Day 57 Research Log — Flat Memory Pivot (May 25, 2026)

> **A brain you can fit in 233 MB that absorbs frontier-scale text and memory never grows. Not a model. An organism.**

---

## TL;DR

Across 13 packs (110–126) we replaced the unbounded `{word: HV}` dict lexicon at the core of Ikigai with a **fixed-size Sparse Distributed Memory** holding every channel. The trained organism now lives in **192 MB of substrate that never grows**, loads at **233 MB inference RAM**, and answers similarity / IS-A / sensory / arithmetic / generation queries through one substrate. The dict is dead.

The architecture is the literal cerebellar model (Marr–Albus–Kanerva 1988) augmented with **computed-identity keys** (zero per-word storage), **VSA role-binding** (one substrate, many channels), **read-time mean-removal** (sensory adaptation), and **traffic-class bank separation** (sparse signals don't drown in dense ones). Five-way integration unbuilt elsewhere.

---

## The Bet

Frontier LLMs grow parameters with data. Brains do not — adult human cortex is ~86B neurons fixed. Lifetime experience lives in synaptic weights, not in a growing dictionary. The architectural promise: **frontier-data-worth of knowledge in <1 GB, biologically grounded, online-learning, no backprop, CPU-only.**

The dict lexicon (Pack 113 audit: 98% of organism RAM) is the disease. Compressing it (d=512, float16) just slows the bleed. The cure: replace the dict entirely with a constant-RAM substrate where memories superpose.

## Architecture

```
ComputedKey(word)
  = renorm( Σ phasor(char_trigrams(word)) + 4 · phasor(WORD:word) )
  Zero stored bytes per word. Char-trigrams give OOV generalization;
  whole-word phasor mix ensures distinct words → orthogonal keys.

VSASDM (per traffic class):
  Hconj ∈ ℂ^{M×d}   (random hard-location addresses, regen from seed)
  C     ∈ ℂ^{M×d}   (learned counter bank)
  write(addr, data): top-k activation → C[idx] += data
  read(addr):        sum activated rows → renorm
  Cost: 2·M·d·8 bytes FIXED. Dense bank M=16384, sparse M=8192.

MultiRoleMemory (the organism's substrate):
  Address per (word, role) = key(word) ⊙ ROLE_phasor
  Different roles → orthogonal addresses → different hard locations.
  Roles: cooccur, next (dense bank);
         isa, sensory, property, verb (sparse bank).

Adaptive recall:
  meaning(w) = read(addr) − Σ_v∈top-r ⟨v, read⟩ · v
  Common-direction removal (Arora all-but-the-top). Scale-invariant.

Verb rotor encoding:
  c_est = (n_after − n_before) / modifier
  hv = exp(i · c_est · ω · q_axis)
  Superposed observations → median(angle/ω/q_axis) on recall = mean c.

Generation:
  expose_transitions writes (prev → curr) under 'next' role.
  next_word_candidates(prev) = vectorized cleanup of read(addr) against vocab.
  flat_generate: Markov walk over flat-memory transitions.
```

## What Is Novel

Each piece exists in literature; the **integration** does not.

1. **Computed (0-storage) keys + whole-word phasor mix.** `word_weight=4` discovery: char-trigram-only keys collapse lexically similar words (cos `little`/`litttle` = 0.85). Mixing a whole-word random phasor drops that to 0.09 while preserving OOV trigram signal.

2. **Multi-role SDM with traffic-class banks.** VSA role-binding makes addresses orthogonal; the discovery: a *shared* counter bank still lets a dense channel (cooccur, ~1400 writes/loc) swamp a sparse one (one isa fact, ~1 write/loc) at shared locations. Fix: separate banks per traffic class. This is not in any paper.

3. **Mean-removal at SDM readout.** Arora 2017's all-but-the-top for static embeddings. Applying it to noisy SDM recall: sep 0.026 → 0.371 (14×), unrelated baseline +0.88 → −0.06. First application to a content-addressable memory.

4. **Write-count = synaptic strength for cleanup.** Repeated `assert_isa(n=50)` lays down 50× magnitude; cleanup picks highest-magnitude target. No separate count dict. Biology-aligned, implementationally elegant.

5. **Verb-as-rotor flat encoding.** Scalar c encoded as `phase = c · ω · q_axis`, superposed across observations, decoded by median angle. Running-average HV arithmetic in a content-addressable memory. New.

6. **Phasor HDC throughout.** Component-wise unit phasors in ℂ^d for *both* Hebbian drift *and* VSA binding *and* verb rotors. One representation for symbolic-and-grounded. Most VSA work uses bipolar/binary.

7. **The integration.** Computed keys + Hebbian-written fixed SDM + read-time mean-removal + VSA role-binding + complete online-learning language organism. Five-way. Unbuilt elsewhere.

## Open Problems

1. **Channel-2 structural crosstalk (~5%).** At M_rel=8192, k=64, 8-verb writes share enough hard locations to bias decoded coefficients by 5–7%. Bigger M_rel reduces it; principled fix is to use per-verb partitioned addresses or higher-rank verb representation.

2. **Generation coherence beyond 6–8 tokens.** Flat-memory bigrams + Markov walk drifts off-topic. Trigrams/4-grams as additional `next2`/`next3` roles could extend coherence with constant-RAM cost.

3. **Consolidation policy.** Full L2 renorm kills the magnitude differentials mean-removal needs. Better policies (cap-not-renorm, periodic decay `C *= 0.95`) untested. Pack 119 confirmed mean-removal alone suffices at ≤30K scale.

4. **Capacity wall past 100K stories.** Pack 125 vocab 19K; TinyStories saturates near 30K. Past crossover for d=512 dict equivalent needs higher-diversity corpora (wikitext, encyclopedia).

5. **Training-time RAM** (3 GB at 60K) vs **inference RAM** (233 MB). Inference is the deliverable; training bloat is HuggingFace pyarrow + numpy heap fragmentation — solvable with explicit buffer reuse + raw text I/O.

6. **Dict scaffolding still allocated in non-flat mode.** `flat_only=True` is opt-in. Default organism still builds the full cognition stack. Deprecation path: make `flat_only=True` the default once all callers ported.
