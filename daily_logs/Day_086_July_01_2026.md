## 2. Analogical Reasoning, By Substrate Algebra

A:B :: C:? is solved with nothing but the phasor algebra. The relation linking A to
B is RECOVERED by unbinding it out of A's holographic signature -- the bundle of
bind(key(relation), key(value)) over A's facts -- and cleaning the result up over
the relation vocabulary by resonance; the same relation is then applied to C by
unbinding it out of C's signature and cleaning up over the value vocabulary. No
relation list, no dictionary search: bind, unbind, cleanup, and the answer is
verified against the store when present. France:Paris::Japan gives Tokyo; the
cross-relation France:Europe::Japan gives Asia; and Vanadium:Metal::Oxygen gives
Gas, because the organism does not parrot "metal" -- it recovers the is-a relation
and applies it to oxygen, returning oxygen's true class. A made-up entity returns
honest silence. Wired as `org.analogy`.

---

## 3. Reverse Derivation

The forward query, run backwards. "What things ARE a metal?" is every is-a and
subclass descendant of metal, found by a reverse walk up the taxonomic links, the
closure derived and never stored -- the same derive-not-store discipline as the
forward transitive reach, only downward, and following mixed instance-of and
subclass-of hops so a two-step member like platinum-through-precious-metal is
found. Attribute relations are excluded structurally, so "what is a paris" is
correctly empty. Wired as `org.what_is_a`.

---

## 4. Self-Directed Curiosity

The organism finds its OWN knowledge gaps. A class is any property shared by two or
more entities -- a high-in-degree value, detected purely from structure; an
entity's peers are the co-members of its classes; and a gap is a relation the peers
have that the entity lacks. The gaps are ranked by the wired CuriosityDrive's
intrinsic novelty and turned into questions the organism could ask. Shown four
metals where three have a density and a luster and one does not, it asks precisely
about the missing density and luster, excludes a property only one peer has, and
never treats its class membership as a gap. Wired as `org.wonder`.

---

## 5. The Autonomous Cognitive Cycle

The first turn from toolbox to organism. `contemplate` runs the reasoning stack
unprompted: it wonders about its own gaps, tries to ANSWER each by derivation and
inheritance -- self-resolving what it can -- inventories what it knows by reverse
derivation, and invents concepts by clustering co-members. To make the self-answer
real across a hierarchy, `inherited_atom` was generalised to climb every
inheritance link together in a breadth-first ascent, so an attribute is inherited
across mixed instance-of and subclass-of hops, and an inheritance rule now
sanctions its own climb without waiting for a separate transitivity rule to be
mined. The cycle is wired into `sleep_consolidate`, so the organism reasons while
it rests, and exposed as `org.think`.

---

## 6. The Life Loop, A Purpose, And Learning From Its Own Thought

Then the heartbeat. `org.live` runs a continuous, unprompted cycle: each tick the
organism PERCEIVES an arriving fact, CONTEMPLATES, and accrues fatigue; when tired
it SLEEPS -- consolidating and dreaming -- and wakes. A lifetime clock counts its
age. It was given an ikigai -- `set_purpose` -- a topic that steers which gaps it
wonders about first; the organism that is named for a reason-for-being finally has
one. And thinking was made to MATTER: when it cannot derive an answer, it ACTS on
its curiosity, forming a low-confidence HYPOTHESIS from peer consensus -- most
metals it knows are heavy, so a new metal is probably heavy too -- stored as a
tagged belief, kept strictly apart from derived facts so a guess never pollutes
ground truth. Across a life the beliefs accumulate; the organism grows from its own
thought.

The loop was then closed against reality. When the organism later PERCEIVES the
truth of something it had guessed, it tests the prediction: a match CONFIRMS the
belief, a mismatch is a SURPRISE that corrects it and logs a prediction error. In a
demonstration the organism met two new metals, guessed both were heavy, had the
first guess confirmed by perception, and was surprised by the second -- a light
metal -- and corrected itself, the belief rewritten from a guess to a fact learned
from being wrong. This is active inference in miniature: predict, be tested,
update. Finally `introspect` lets the organism reflect on itself and say, faithfully
and from its real state, who it is -- its age, its purpose, what it knows, the
hypotheses it still wants to confirm, the thing it was wrong about and corrected,
and what it wonders now.

## 7. Discipline, Honesty, And The Gate

A midday audit against the founder's hardest question -- did any of this bend a law
of physics, computation, or information theory -- came back clean: the answerable
multiplier is transitive closure computed not stored (no Shannon violation, and the
sub-megabyte shortfall was reported honestly); learning without backpropagation uses
established non-gradient methods; flat query cost is content addressing; capacity
respects the Kanerva bound. The answer-producing reasoning is genuinely substrate
work -- analogy by unbind and resonance, concept membership by resonance,
inheritance and reverse closure by derive-chaining. The drives and orchestration
(curiosity, pursuit, the life loop, introspection) are honest meta-cognition over
the store, and are named as such rather than dressed up as reasoning. Every code
change held the regression gate -- twenty of twenty-one capitals, the arithmetic
ring eight of eight, calibration abstaining, all ten audited capabilities live --
and eight new gates in `experiments/nl/day86_*` cover analogy, reverse derivation,
curiosity, the autonomous cycle, the life loop, predictive learning, and
introspection.
