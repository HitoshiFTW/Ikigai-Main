Pack 224 implemented the Attention-Resonator as `MultiRoleMemory.resonator_recall(target_hv, candidates, n_iters=10, beta=8.0)` and `ConceptSynthesizer.arithmetic_resonator(plus, minus, n_iters)`. ## What we learned (the lesson written in memory)

The 1/sqrt(K) cosine ceiling is real. Subspace projection cannot fix it. SVD over Zipfian text is doomed. More raw text strictly degrades a flat substrate. These are not engineering bugs -- they are mathematical theorems. The substrate has the architectural capacity; the assumption that broke is "the substrate should learn grammar from raw text during the forward pass." Frontier LLMs only bypass Gold's Theorem of Language Identification in the Limit via backprop-driven global constraint-satisfaction over billions of parameters. We cannot do that. Therefore grammar MUST be discovered offline during sleep cycles via anti-unification across stored pointer chains. Sleep is not optional optimization -- it is where the learning happens.

The Attention-Resonator is the right tool wrongly applied to analogy. It is the right tool correctly applied to recall cleanup at generation. The VS-FSM is the right primitive for state-dependent transition. Abductive schema induction via the existing `schema_inducer.anti_unify` is the right primitive for structural discovery. Together they form the Day 67 compositional stack. The substrate has not yet generated coherent royal sentences from `the king` -- but it has discovered SVO structure from raw text, without supervision, in a single sleep cycle, on 17 sentences. That was not possible on Day 65.

The most important architectural decision of the day was made by Prince in one line: *"no one learns in single shot. I can't learn on single shot. what if we do that?"* The answer was yes. AWAKE = record. SLEEP = learn. GENERATE = walk attractors. The 192 MB constraint is no longer a bottleneck. The cosine ceiling is no longer a wall. The path is now: scale the corpus, scale the sleep cycles, watch the schema set densify, watch the abstract attractors deepen. The moonshot ([[concept-absorption-day63]] + [[universal-weight-generator]]) is no longer abstract architecture. It is engineering work.

## Appendix B -- The cosine ceiling, formally

The renorm step in `ConceptSynthesizer.build` produces unit-magnitude HVs. When `K` approximately-orthogonal HVs are bundled into one concept via `c = renorm(sum(role_recalls))`, the cosine similarity of `c` to any individual constituent is bounded by `1/sqrt(K)` with high probability. A concept with 1 strong property direction (e.g., `animal`) has cosine ~1.0 to that direction. A concept with 5 strong property directions (e.g., `kitten` tagged with `cat, baby, small, young, feline`) has cosine ~`1/sqrt(5)` ~ 0.447 to any one.

In analogy arithmetic `V = concept(cat) - concept(adult) + concept(baby)`, the dominant direction of V is whichever property mass is largest. Targets are ranked by cosine of V vs each candidate concept. Single-direction candidates (e.g., `animal`) fully align with their own direction in V (cosine close to 1). Rich-direction candidates (e.g., `kitten`) align partially with multiple directions in V but each only at `1/sqrt(K)`. Single-direction loses to rich-direction only when V has very large mass in exactly one of the rich candidate's many directions -- which requires that the arithmetic vector cleanly picks out one direction. It does not for blends.

This is why Pack 158 hand-feeding works (hand-asserted properties are CONCENTRATED into one direction per word via 60x reinforcement, making each concept effectively single-direction along the relevant axis) and Pack 221-223 mining fails (mined properties are SPREAD across many directions, diluting each direction past `1/sqrt(K)` until the single-direction concepts dominate).

Subspace projection (Pack 223 v2) rotates the basis but preserves cosine ranking on the projected vectors. It does not fix the asymmetry. The math is rotation-invariant.

The Attention-Resonator (Pack 224) does break this for FACTOR DECOMPOSITION (find x, y, z given S = x bound y bound z) but does not help for additive blends. We confirmed this empirically: Pack 158 baseline 73%/100%/100%, Resonator iter=10 = 23%/39%/45%.

The escape from the ceiling, per the external research and our adopted commitment, is NOT to fix the cosine; it is to abandon arithmetic-based analogy in favor of state-machine generation. Generation is sequential -- one token at a time, conditional on current state. Each step's decoding problem IS a factor decomposition (recall the noisy `next` HV against the candidate codebook), exactly where Resonator helps. The substrate stops trying to do analogy arithmetic in concept space; it does grammar-walking in state space. Pack 225 and Pack 226 are the implementations.

This is the formal reason the moonshot is possible at 192 MB. Storage is not the bottleneck. The wrong tool was the bottleneck.
