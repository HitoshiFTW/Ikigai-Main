## 1. Objective

Day 82 proved the reasoning edge -- a phone-sized organism out-reasoning a frontier
model on equal-knowledge multi-hop chains -- and named the language front-end as
the bridge from that narrow victory to general use. Day 83 set out to build that
front door and then to make the organism worthy of standing behind it. Two
distinct bodies of work resulted. The first was the natural-language reader: a way
for the organism to take ordinary messy text, comprehend it into facts on its own
terms, and answer questions about it, all without a single authored template or
relation list. The second, larger, was a reckoning the founder had been pressing
toward for days -- that the organism had accreted roughly a hundred and twenty
cognition modules over its life, many half-wired, some duplicated, a few dead or
quietly cheating, and that the time had come to read every one of them and forge
the lot into a single organism with no modes and no disabled parts. The standing
requirement framed both halves: the win is to match frontier knowledge by scale
and do the best reasoning in the world on top of it, and neither is served by a
codebase that runs differently depending on a flag.

## 2. The Template-Free Holographic Reader

The founder's vision was precise and demanding: read any sentence, ask back, and
let the answer fall out by resonance -- no templates, no relation tables, no
grammar, no stopword lists. Pack 333 built exactly that as
`ikigai/cognition/holo_read.py`. The reader binds every word into a context
hypervector and accumulates it into a sparse distributed memory; asking a question
is unbinding the cue from that memory, and the answer is whatever survives the
cleanup. It is backed by an SDM so recall holds to two thousand sentences at full
fidelity. The question side asks in plain English with no hole markers, no
wh-word list, no stopword removal, and no stemmer -- it treats the question as an
order-free bag, recovers word identity through the substrate's own trigram
morphology, and treats the answer simply as the cue that is missing. It
disambiguates a single subject across multiple relations and answers
honest-unknown when it has nothing. It is exposed as `org.ask_holo`.

---

## 3. Emergent Comprehension Feeding The Derive Engine

A reader that only remembers is an episodic store; the founder's standard is that
multi-hop facts must be DERIVED, never stored. Pack 334 added emergent atom
extraction: a relation is recognised as a high-document-frequency recurring
connective with a data-scaled cut, with no lists, templates, or grammar, and the
extracted triples are handed to the EXISTING composition engine through
`org.comprehend`. The holographic reader thus became the episodic front door --
which it is legitimate to store -- while the derive engine remained the semantic
store, and a four-hop fact read from prose is derived, not kept. Pack 335 closed
the loop with `org.ask_derive`: a plain-English question is parsed emergently
(entity by novelty, relation by recurrence and trigram morphology, with no wh-word
or relation lists) and answered by the semantic derive engine rather than the
episodic store, multi-hop and derived-not-stored. Packs 336 through 339 hardened
the reader against the real world: consecutive low-frequency argument tokens merge
into a single multi-word entity span emergently, so "buenos aires" survives as one
thing; a structural relation classifier replaced the frequency-only one, fixing
the hub-entity misclassification that prose exposed; a native dopamine
reinforcement path was added so the organism discovers RARE relations from reward
when their frequency falls below the recurrence cut; and hop-depth itself was made
emergent, read from the number of relation mentions in the question rather than
passed in.

## 4. Real Data Through The Pipe -- The Scale Multiplier

With the front-end built data-free on invented entities, real knowledge was poured
through. Packs 340 and 341 ran one million ConceptNet edges in roughly ten minutes
on a single CPU core, producing six hundred and thirty-seven thousand atoms over
four hundred and seventy-five thousand entities in a 4.36-megabyte kernel with
eleven discovered transitive rules. The honest accounting is that the full
answerable-fact count -- pairwise comparisons, forward and reverse closure, and
derived chains -- reaches two hundred and twenty-five million, a 353-fold
multiplier over the stored kernel, which projects to roughly nineteen megabytes
for a billion facts on this data. The sub-megabyte-per-billion claim was NOT met
on ConceptNet, which is shallow and sparse with no clean inheritance, and this was
recorded plainly; sub-megabyte needs dense data with deep subclass chains and
inheriting attributes, which is what the staged Wikidata N-triples pipeline is for.
That pipeline was built and proven on a synthetic dump, with bz2 support added, and
left ready for the founder's downloading forty-three-gigabyte truthy dump to feed
it the following day.

## 5. The Audit -- Reading One Hundred And Twenty-One Modules

The second half of the day answered a demand the founder had sharpened over the
prior days: the organism must be ONE organism. Not a lean benchmark mode and a
full mode; not a constructor full of classes that are silently `None`; not two
modules that do the same thing with one left to rot. Call the organism and
everything runs, every function meaningful, nothing dead and nothing cheating. The
method was the founder's own and non-negotiable -- read every file line by line,
experiment with it, judge its real value before touching it, because a
zero-reference module is as likely to be valuable-but-unwired as it is to be
junk, and value must never be lost by chance. All one hundred and twenty-one
cognition files were read this way and a durable ledger of verdicts was kept.

The top-line result was reassuring: the organism is healthy. The production
reasoning stack -- the unified substrate, the residue-number ring whose arithmetic
is genuine phasor-and-remainder algebra rather than a Python calculator behind a
facade, the derive engine, the rule miner, the in-context fact cache, the
data-free calibration layer, and the dopamine reinforcement gate -- was verified
clean and non-cheating end to end. Every reasoning operation is a substrate
operation. The reasoning pillars all exist natively. The rot, where it existed,
was entirely old and unwired -- superseded by the wins that came after it.

## 6. The Execution -- Deletions And Rewires

The audit's decisions were executed as gated batches, each one backed up, applied,
and then checked against a regression gate of twenty-one capitals and nine
arithmetic problems plus the natural-language gates before proceeding. Ten modules
were deleted. The dead and superseded went first: a Python n-gram stack and a
Day-55 generation orchestrator long replaced by the substrate's own n-gram
machinery and the data-free frame-relaxation generator. Then the cheating and the
abandoned: a code-generation module carrying a four-hundred-and-eighty-line canned
corpus of HumanEval solutions -- answer-baking, the exact violation the founder
forbids -- and its execution wrapper; a deprecated keyword-routing chatbot that
still contained the forbidden eval-based arithmetic. Then the deprecated trio, the
single biggest dead weight at roughly thirty-seven hundred lines: a GSM8K-tuned
heuristic verifier, a legacy reasoner that did arithmetic in a Python dictionary
rather than the substrate, and the 2,705-line transformer-to-substrate weight-bake
compiler from the abandoned mission, read in full before deletion to confirm no
salvageable primitive was lost. The founder's named invention, the unitary-dilation
spectral projector, was carefully decoupled and preserved, as were two other
protected pieces of intellectual property. The public generation and reasoning
entry points -- `org.generate`, the dialogue loop, `org.ask`, and the
statement-parsing path -- were rewired onto the frame-relaxation generator and the
general reasoner and the emergent holographic comprehension before their legacy
implementations were removed. Finally one hundred and eighty-four obsolete
experiment files from before Day 71 were deleted, clearing the old weight-bake era
entirely.

## 7. Fixing The Broken, Wiring The Dormant

Three methods were found degenerate and fixed. Two convergence routines were
no-ops on bipolar vectors -- a half-step nudge cannot flip a plus-or-minus-one
sign -- and were rebuilt as flip-fraction updates that actually move toward their
target; a third, a free-energy action selector, always returned the same action
because it scored every choice against a single scalar, and was rebuilt to score
each action by the free-energy component it actually reduces, making it
state-dependent.

The larger work was wiring the dormant gold -- the recurring finding of the audit
was that most modules' core methods were never called; only their statistics paths
ran. Twelve capabilities were wired so they now run and were each proven by a new
gate. The planning pillar -- a free-energy-guided tree search with backtracking
over the causal world model -- is now queryable as `org.plan` and finds real paths
to a goal; this is the native planner the earlier Python-backtracker experiments
should have used. Proof-carrying generation attaches a verifiable derivation chain
to every derived answer through `org.ask_derive_proof`, rejecting a tampered chain
and abstaining when verification fails -- the anti-hallucination thesis made real.
The adversarial immune system can now be armed and detects prompt-injection
patterns; the logical fixed-point reasoner explains its derivations; substrate-native
credit assignment, theory-of-mind agreement and false-belief reasoning,
metacognitive drift flagging, bio-forgetting during sleep, curiosity-driven action
selection, belief-contradiction healing, counterfactual action choice, and
time-as-a-role temporal indexing were all wired and gated.

## 8. De-Hardcoding, Collapse, And One Dimension

The single hardcoded list remaining on the production reasoning path -- the founder
had flagged it many times -- was the set of English arithmetic words that gated the
substrate-arithmetic path. It was eliminated. Operator recognition is now
structural, through the arithmetic symbols and the two-or-more-numbers rule that
already covered ordinary arithmetic, or emergent, through the substrate's learned
operator detector, with no authored word list anywhere on the path. The redundant
double-instantiation -- five modules built once unconditionally, then clobbered to
nothing in lean mode and rebuilt in full mode -- was collapsed to a single build,
and four dead duplicate instances were dropped. The legacy modules that had been
hardcoded to a 2048-dimension space separate from the 400-dimension substrate were
unified onto the substrate's own dimension, after an investigation confirmed the
2048 was a capacity choice and not the reasoning lever, so the organism now lives
in one dimension centred on its residue-ring, cache, and memory core; the
prime-offset permutation algebra was made dimension-adaptive in the process so it
no longer crashes at small dimensions. The being's word-lexicon, which had minted
its own random hypervectors divorced from the substrate, was migrated to seed every
word from the substrate's deterministic computed key, so the dictionary now shares
one identity space with the substrate while keeping its own drift, dream, and
lifetime on top -- the dictionary-to-hyperdimensional-computing supersession the
founder had named as the canonical example.

## 9. One Organism, Everything On

The final step removed the mode switch entirely. The `flat_only` flag is now a
no-op, kept only so old call-sites do not error; every cognition module is built
unconditionally. Calling `IkigaiOrganism()` with no arguments builds the whole
organism -- twenty-two of twenty-two modules live, none disabled -- in under a
second, loads the production body in two seconds, and reasons. There is no longer a
lean organism and a full organism; there is one organism, and everything in it
runs.
