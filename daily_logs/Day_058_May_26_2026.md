## The new architecture (additions vs Day 57)

### Vision channel -- modality blindness made concrete

Random projection encoder (`P @ pixels -> phase -> exp(i*phase)`) at d=512. Each image becomes a `complex64` HV of the same shape as a word key. Wrote each (image, label) pair under the new `class` role in the sparse relational bank. Classification = cleanup against `key(label)` candidates.

The pre-Pack-134 encoder collapsed at 28x28 input (phases wrapped many times, within-class cos == between-class cos == 0). The 3-line fix:

```python
v = pixels / ||pixels||                # L2-normalize for data-range invariance
P = N(0, bandwidth=2)^(d, in_dim)      # phase std becomes ~2 rad regardless of in_dim
hv = exp(i * P @ v)
```

Result: 92% on Split-MNIST 28x28 with zero forgetting across 5 sequential tasks. CNN baseline collapsed to 10% by task 5. The substrate's architectural advantage in continual learning is quantified.

### Generation engine -- the novel piece

`ikigai/cognition/generation_engine.py` exposes `IkigaiOrganism.cogitate(prompt, max_tokens, think_steps=3, momentum=0.7, thought_gamma=4.0, ngram_weights=(0.2, 0.4, 0.4))`.

Three decisions in one engine, none of which appear together in the published literature:

1. **Thought is a phasor HV in the substrate's own address space.** Not a context window. Not hidden activations. A single complex64 vector of dimension d=512 that evolves over generation steps.

2. **Think and speak are decoupled.**
   - Think step: `addr = thought * ROLE_cooccur; thought = momentum * thought + (1 - momentum) * sdm.read(addr)`. Associative walk through flat memory.
   - Speak step: combined n-gram backoff (bigram + trigram + 4-gram) scoring + softmax sampling weighted by thought-alignment: `score_w = base_w * exp(gamma * cos(key(w), thought))`.
   - Loop: K think steps, 1 speak token, repeat.

3. **No context window.** Thought carries the running semantic anchor across arbitrary length. Per-token cost is O(1). No KV cache. No O(N^2) attention. Memory RAM does not grow with output length.

Empirical (Pack 135): n=50 / n=500 / n=2000 token generations all run at ~67-150 tok/s on CPU with substrate at 192 MB the entire time. Mid-generation fact injection (`org.assert_isa('zerg', 'creature'); org.few_shot_learn(...)`) is immediately reflected in subsequent tokens -- no retrain, no RAG.

### Trigram and 4-gram channels via permute-then-bind

`MultiRoleMemory._ngram_ctx_hv(ctx_tokens)` constructs an ordered-context HV by cyclic-shifting each context-token's key by `(n-1-i)` positions then binding. Distinct n-grams produce distinct addresses regardless of token-set overlap -- "the cat sat" and "cat the sat" map to different memory locations. Pack 136 adds `next2` (trigram) and `next3` (4-gram) roles to the dense bank. Cost: zero new substrate. Three channels share the same 128 MB cooccur+next bank via role-binding.

`combined_ngram_candidates(last_tokens, weights=(w_bi, w_tri, w_4))` does weighted backoff scoring. Default weights `(0.2, 0.4, 0.4)` tilt higher-order while keeping bigram as backoff for sparse n-grams. The generation engine's `speak_step` now uses this scorer.

### RapidTrainer -- the path to scale

The baseline `expose_cooccur` + `expose_transitions` loop paid one M=16384 matmul cost per write per sentence. At ~50 sentences per Wikipedia article, that's ~50 matmuls per article. RapidTrainer batches across K sentences (default K=128), aggregates per-unique-key once, fires a single `locs_batch` matmul per role per flush. Combined with a stopword filter (drops the/a/and/is/was/etc.), training accelerated 7.5x on a toy corpus and ~3.8x on real Wikipedia. The write semantics are preserved -- substrate, recall quality, n-gram coverage are all identical to baseline.

This unblocks Pack 137. 200 articles in 67.7s. 10,000 articles projected at ~57 minutes. Full Simple English Wikipedia (~200K articles) projected at one overnight run.

### Ablation harness -- the answer to Imy

A Discord reviewer (Imy) made the right critique: the bio stack carries 20+ interacting components and any of them could be cosmetic. Pack 140 builds a harness that toggles individual mechanisms off via `exec()` patching of `ikigai.py` source. The canonical organism is never permanently modified -- project rule #1 holds. Each ablation runs 300 ticks of the full simulation and records per-tick `pred_error`, `energy`, `cortisol`, `sleeping`, `dopamine`.

| config            | PE     | E     | cort  | sleep | da    |
|-------------------|--------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| baseline          | 0.008  | 0.503 | 0.168 | 0.000 | 0.545 |
| no_cortisol       | 0.009  | 0.498 | **0.000** | 0.000 | **0.754** |
| no_sleep          | 0.008  | 0.517 | 0.173 | **0.000** | 0.407 |
| no_dopamine_supp  | 0.009  | 0.554 | 0.171 | 0.000 | **0.284** |
| no_arousal        | 0.008  | 0.509 | 0.173 | 0.000 | 0.412 |
| no_l23_recovery   | 0.009  | 0.510 | 0.169 | 0.000 | 0.578 |

Killing cortisol drives dopamine up 38%, confirming the cortisol-DA coupling literature in our model. Removing the DA-suppression term paradoxically drops DA to 0.284 -- other modulators compensate via a different pathway. At 300 ticks no config reaches sleep onset (adenosine ramp is too slow for this horizon); a 2,000-tick Pack 141 will surface sleep-related deltas.

## The Imy correspondence

Three Discord exchanges, three different teaching moments.

**First reply (the standard pitch):** he asked for an overview; we shipped a tight 14-bullet description and the repo link. The reply that came back named the right concern -- too many bio mechanisms stacked on each other, interactions hard to analyse, hard to know which carry weight.

**Second reply (the right reframe):** he pointed out that every "mechanism" is a chosen abstraction the modeler defined, even if it's lifted from neuroscience papers. The conceptual rebuttal -- that biology is the source rather than the rule -- collapses under this lens. Every choice of LIF over Hodgkin-Huxley, every cortisol decay constant, every E/I balance equation IS a rule the modeler invented. The honest answer is to concede the framing.

**Third reply (the suggestion that became Pack 140):** he proposed building bottom-up from a generic RNN, adding each mechanism with proper ablation, instead of top-down from biology. The full version of that is a multi-month project; the immediate version is a harness that toggles each existing mechanism off and measures the delta. That's Pack 140.

The harness is now in the repo. Imy can now run the rigor he asked for.

## What's still missing

- **Generation quality at this corpus size.** 200 Wikipedia articles populates trigram coverage at 11,873 contexts and 4-gram at 11,093 -- enough to prove the architecture works, not enough for coherent multi-sentence generation. Cogitate output on `the city of` produces real Wikipedia vocab but disconnected sequences. Pack 141 candidate: scale to 10K-100K articles.
- **Sleep dynamics not visible in 300-tick ablations.** All Pack 140 ablations show sleep_frac = 0.000 at this horizon. The adenosine ramp needs ~700-1000 ticks for sleep onset.
- **PCA / RMSNorm style additions to the substrate.** No PCA pre-encoder needed at current scale (Pack 134 conclusion), but at 100K+ vocab it may matter.
- **Benchmark harness for MMLU / GSM8K / HumanEval.** The flatmem bench harness in `github/flatmem/bench/` covers MNIST. Real-text benchmarks are the next frontier.
